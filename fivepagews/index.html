First-time zucchini bread!

This morning I made zucchini bread for the first time with my homegrown zucchini. It was much simplier than I had anticipated. All I had to do according to my recipe was combine dry ingredients to my wet ingredients. It did take quite a bit of time to bake (1 hour), but is so worth it! The next time I make this I am going to add 1 cup of chocolate chips or 3 tablespoons of honey to make it sweeter.
