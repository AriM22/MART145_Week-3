Challah

Ingredients

  • 2/3 cup water
  • 2 eggs
  • 2 tbsp. vegetable oil
  • 2 tbsp. sugar
  • 1 tsp. salt
  • 3 cups bread flour
  • 1 1/2 tsp. active dry yeast
  • 1 egg, beaten

Directions

  1. Put the water, eggs, oil, sugar, salt, flour, and yeast in bread machine.
  2. Remove dough from machine and divide into 3 pieces.
  3. Role each piece into a rope about 14 inches ling and braid on a greased baking sheet.
  4. Cover and rise for about 45 minutes.
  5. Brush top with egg glaze and bake in a preheated 350F oven for 45 minutes.
Cinnamon Swirl Bread

Ingredients

  • 2 cups flour
  • 1/2 tsp. salt
  • 1 tsp. cinnamon
  • 1 tsp. baking powder
  • 1/2 tsp. baking soda
  • 8 tbsp. butter, melted
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 1 tsp. vanilla
  • 2 large eggs
  • 3/4 cup sourdough discard
  • Cinnamon Swirl: 1 tbsp. cinnamon + 1/2 cup brown sugar

Directions

  1. Preheat oven to 350F.
  2. Grease loaf pan/line with parchment paper
  3. In a large mixing bowl, whisk the melted butter, sugar, vanilla, eggs, and sourdough.
  4. In a seperate bowl, combine the dry ingredients: flour, salt, cinnamon, baking powder, and baking soda.
  5. Gradually add the bowl of dry ingredients into the wet ingredients, then mix until just combined
  6. In a small bowl create the cinnamon swirl by mixing the cinnamon and brown sugar
  7. Add half of the batter to the pan, then sprinkle 1/2 of swirl mixture. Add remaining batter then sprinkle the remaining
  8. Using a knife or toothpick, make swirls to spread the cinnamon sugar
  9. Bake for 1 hour
Whole Wheat Honey Loaf

Ingredients

  • 1 3/4 cup water

  • 3 tbsp. oil
  • 4 tbsp. honey
  • 2 1/3 cups whole wheat flour

  2. Choose the whole wheat setting on the machine and let the machine run.
  3. Take bread out and enjoy!

Bread Flour Chocolate Chip Cookies

Ingredients

  • 1 cup butter, cold and cut into cubes
  • 1 cup brown sugar
  • 1/2 cup sugar
  • 2 eggs, room temperature
  • 2 tsp. vanilla extract
  • 3 cups bread flour
  • 1 tbsp. cornstarch
  • 1 tsp. baking soda
  • 1 tsp. salt
  • 2 cups semi-sweet chocolate chips (I use Ghiradelli or Kirkland)
  • 2 cups milk chocolate chips(I use Ghiradelli or Kirkland)

Directions

  1. Cream butter and sugar in a stand mixer until light.
  2. Add eggs one at a time. Make sure to beat well inbetween so everything is incorporated. Add the vanilla.
  3. Whisk the flour, cornstarch, baking soda, and salt in a seperate mixing bowl.
  4. Put all of the dry ingredients into the stand mixer that has the wet ingredients. Turn mixer on medium until the flour is just combined. (Don't overmix)
  5. Add chocolate chips and stir on low
  6. Scoop 2 tablespoons (I also measure 1.8 grams per cookie if I need them to look similar.) of dough onto a lined baking sheet. Spread them 2 inches apart.
  7. Bake for 8 minutes. They may be a bit under baked, but they will finish cooking from the residual heat.
  8. Allow cookies to rest for 15 minutes before removing them from the cookie sheet
Peanut Butter Cookies

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup shortening
  • 1/2 cup peanut butter
  • 1/2 cup granulated sugar
  • 1/2 cup brown sugar, packed
  • 1 egg
  • 1 1/4 cups Gold Medal Flour
  • 1/2 tsp. baking powder
  • 3/4 tsp. baking soda
  • 1/4 tsp. salt

Directions

  1. Mix together the shortening, peanut butter, sugars, and egg.
  2. Blend all dry ingredients; stir into shortening mixture.
  3. Chill the dough for a minimum of 30 minutes.
  4. Heat oven to 375F. Roll dough in 1 1/4" balls. Place 3" apart on lightly greased baking sheet. Using a fork dipped in flour, flatten dough with a crisscross style.
  5. Bake for 10 to 12 minutes
No Bake Cookies

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup salted butter
  • 1 3/4 cups granulated sugar
  • 1/3 cup unsweetened cocoa powder
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 2/3 cup creamy peanut butter
  • 3 cups quick oats

Directions

  1. Using parchment paper, line two baking sheets.
  2. Combine butter, sugar, cocoa, and milk in a medium saucepan.
  3. Stir frequently over medium heat until it reaches a full boil.
  4. Boil the mixture for 1 minutes without stirring
  5. Remove from heat, then immediately add in vanilla, peanut butter, and quick oats.
  6. Once stirred and combined, scoop approximately 2 Tbsp of the mixture onto the lined sheets for each cookie.
  7. Allow the cookies to rest at room temperature for about 30 minutes or set them in the refrigerater for 20 minutes.
  8. After they are set you can store them at room temperature in an airtight container.
Gingersnaps

Ingredients

  • 3/4 cup shortening
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 1/4 cup light molasses
  • 1 beaten egg
  • 2 cups sifted flour
  • 2 tsp. baking soda
  • 1/4 tsp. salt
  • 1 tsp. cinnamon
  • 1 tsp. cloves
  • 1 tsp. ginger

Directions

  1. Cream shortening and sugar
  2. Add molasses and egg. Beat well
  3. Sift dry ingreidents into a mixing bowl. Add to creamed mixture, mix well.
  4. Roll dough into approximately 1 1/2" balls. Dip into sugar.
  5. Place on greased sheet pan. Bake in a 375F oven for 7 minutes.

Old Fashioned Apple Pie

Ingredients

  • Pastry for 2-crust pie (I use Martha Stewart's)
  • 3/4 cup sugar (this may vary with tartness of apples)
  • 2 Tbsp. flour
  • 1 Tsp. cinnamon
  • 1/8 Tsp. nutmeg
  • 1/4 Tsp. salt
  • 6 cups sliced peeled apples
  • 2 Tbsp. butter or margarine

Directions

  1. Mix together the sugar, flour, cinnamon, nutmeg, and salt.
  2. Cover and mix the apple slices in the sugar mixture.
  3. Place the apple slices in the pastry-line 9" pie pan.
  4. Dot butter throughout the top of the apples.
  5. Put top crust on and crimp the edges. Cut vents on the top.
  6. Brush an egg wash on the top. Heat oven to 425F and bake for 50-60 minutes.
Chocolate Pudding Pie

Ingredients

  • 1 cup sugar
  • 1/2 tbsp. flour
  • 2 tbsp. cocoa powder
  • 1 pinch salt
  • 2 cups milk
  • 2 egg yolks
  • 1 tsp. vanilla
  • 1 tsp. butter
  • 1 baked pastry crust

Directions

  1. Stir dry ingredients in a medium saucepan.
  2. Add 1/3 cup of milk and stir.
  3. Add uneaten egg yolks to the mixture.
  4. Add rest of milk.
  5. Cook at medium high and stir constantly. Boil until thick.
  6. Turn off heat and add vanilla and butter.
  7. Put pudding in pastry crust and set in refrigerater.

Randomly Baked

These are a few of my other recipes that don't fit into a category, but they are just too good to not share!

Vanilla Pecan Granola

Ingredients

  • 4 cups old fashioned oats
  • 1 1/2 cups pecans/nuts of choice (I use 3/4 cup pecans and 3/4 cup pumpkin seeds)
  • 2 tbsp. chia seeds
  • 1 tsp. salt
  • 1/2 tsp. cinnamon
  • 1/2 cup melted coconut oil
  • 1/2 cup melted honey
  • 1 tsp. vanilla
  • 2/3 cup dried cranberries/dried fruit of choice

Directions

  1. Preheat oven to 350F.
  2. Mix dry ingredients in bowl.
  3. Add oil, honey, vanilla, and fruit into the dry ingredients.
  4. Dump onto a greased jelly roll pan.
  5. Press firm with spoon or hand.
  6. Bake for 22 minutes or until golden brown. Let cool then store in an airtight container (I use 2 mason jars)
Christmas Toffee

Ingredients

  • 1 package Honey Graham Crackers
  • 14 ounces milk chocolate
  • 1 cup butter
  • 1 cup brown sugar
  • 1 cup chooped pecans

Directions

  1. Line a 9x13" pan with parchment paper. Preheat oven to 350F.
  2. Place the graham crackers on the bottom of pan to cover it.
  3. To make the toffee layer: Melt the butter in a large saucepan. Add the pecans and brown sugar, and stir constantly. Boil the mixture for 5 minutes, stirring constantly.
  4. Pour the toffee over the graham crackers and spread into an even layer. Bake this for 7 minutes.
