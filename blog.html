Overproofing... NEVER

September 10, 2026

It may not work again, but if you overproof sourdough don't worry because if you put it in a small loaf pan it will be just fine. Today I woke up late which resulted in not sticking my loaf in the oven until ten hours later. Instead of throwing it away, I chose to experiment by preheating my oven like normal then putting the dough in my banana bread pan. When I took it out it was a bit crusty, however it tasted just fine! Key Takeaway: Try it even if something might not work out the way you want.
